Coil Out: Snake Puzzle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What the game keeps

The game keeps the level you reached, your star rating for each level, which short tips you have already seen, and your sound setting. On our own website this is kept in your browser storage on your device. On a game portal it is kept through the portal's save feature. We do not receive it.

What the game does not collect

The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location. It has no account and no sign-in. It does not use analytics or cookies. We do not sell any data.

Ads and game portals

If you play on a game portal (for example Playgama) the portal can show ads, including optional videos you choose to watch for a hint or an extra life, and can collect data under its own privacy policy. Please read the policy of that portal.

Children

The game is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Delete your data

To delete all game data, clear the site data for this game in your browser settings. On a portal, use the portal's own controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
